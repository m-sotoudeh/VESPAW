# Code of conduct

VESPAW follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: be respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests and all
other project spaces; harassment and personal attacks are not accepted.

Problems can be reported confidentially to the maintainer,
Mohsen Sotoudeh (mohsen.sotoudeh@kit.edu). Reports are handled
discreetly, and the maintainer may remove contributions or block
participants who do not follow these rules.
